# Sucker for Love: Crush Landing — Preview research

Research checked: 2026-09-01

## Confirmed game identity

- Title: Sucker for Love: Crush Landing
- Steam App ID: 3848900
- Developer: Akabaka
- Publisher: Black Lantern Collective
- Steam release date: August 31, 2026
- Steam store page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3848900/Sucker_for_Love_Crush_Landing/

## Confirmed player-facing facts

The Steam store description confirms a horror-comedy visual novel in which a
sentient, shapeshifting comet named Hheily crash-lands in the player's
apartment. The player is asked to feed Hheily's fire, with the story framed
around an interplanetary conflict between light and dark.

The same official page confirms colorful puzzles, multiple endings based on
actions, fully voiced story-rich dialogue, hand-crafted music and artwork,
and 360-degree hand-drawn 2D apartment environments. The minimum listed PC
requirements are Windows 10 and 4 GB RAM.
